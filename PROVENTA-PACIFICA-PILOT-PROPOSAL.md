# PROVENTA — INSTITUTIONAL PILOT PROPOSAL
## Prepared for Pacifica Companies (Ahmedabad & Gujarat Operations)

**Platform:** PROVENTA — Construction Procurement & Intelligence Platform (`https://proventa.in`)  
**Tagline:** *Source better. Compare smarter. Procure with confidence.*  
**Pilot Scope:** One Buyer Organisation &bull; One Active Project &bull; One Core Material Category  

---

## 1. Pilot Objective

Demonstrate measurable operational control, transparent landed-cost comparability, and auditable governance on a single active construction material procurement cycle — without disrupting existing ERP or accounting workflows.

### Recommended Pilot Configuration
- **Buyer Organisation:** Pacifica Companies (Gujarat Procurement Division)
- **Pilot Project:** One active residential or commercial high-rise site in Ahmedabad / Gandhinagar
- **Material Category:** TMT Steel Reinforcement Bars (Fe-550D, IS 1786:2008, BIS + MTC Mandatory) **or** OPC 53 / PPC Cement
- **Supplier Panel:** 3–5 verified authorised distributors / mills (including existing incumbent vendor + qualified regional alternatives)

---

## 2. End-to-End Pilot Workflow

1. **Requirement Lock:** Capture site quantity, delivery schedule, acceptable primary brands, and mandatory BIS/MTC specifications.
2. **Structured RFQ Dispatch:** Invite shortlisted verified suppliers privately; competitor pricing remains strictly confidential.
3. **Intelligent Landed-Cost Comparison:** Normalize all quotes to exact site-landed cost (`Base + GST + Freight + Loading + Unloading + Insurance - Discounts`) in integer paise. Any non-equivalent grade or secondary mill substitution is flagged explicitly as `NON-EQUIVALENT SPECIFICATION`.
4. **Dual-Control Approval & Protected Award:** Route recommendation through Procurement Head and Finance approvers with separation of duties, generating an auditable Purchase Order.
5. **Dispatch, GRN & Three-Way Reconciliation:** Track vehicle dispatch, e-way bill, site weighbridge Goods Receipt Note (GRN), and automated `PO + GRN + Invoice` matching.
6. **Procurement Savings Assessment Report:** Deliver a transparent executive report separating **Potential Savings** (quotation vs. historical baseline) from **Verified Realised Savings** (backed strictly by executed PO + GRN + Invoice).

---

## 3. Demonstration Mode & Evidence Integrity

- An interactive demonstration workspace is live at `/pilot/pacifica` (API: `/api/procurement/pilot/pacifica`).
- All demonstration records are explicitly labelled `SAMPLE DATA — ISOLATED FROM PRODUCTION` and are never commingled with genuine buyer records.
- **No Guaranteed Savings Policy:** PROVENTA does not guarantee percentage cost reductions. Realised commercial outcomes depend on live mill pricing, credit terms, freight distance, and specification compliance.
